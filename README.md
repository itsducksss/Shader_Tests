# "Wine"/ Liquid Shader Test Preview

Video displays preview for liquid simulation in Unity.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7a278584-f12a-4f7f-bd63-52742153966d

Disclaimer:
To see the full liquid simulation play the Unity Project

You can change:
- The colour of the Liquid
- The mesh/ affected object affected by the shader
- The amount of Sine waves for the simulatuion (The frequency)
- The amplitude of the Sine Waves (How high and low the waves move between)
- The recovery speed of the liquid (Which would change the percieved viscocity of the liquid)
- How full the container is (By changing the float value)
